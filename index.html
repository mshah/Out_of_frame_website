The central question
“A photograph can preserve the person who fired the shot. What happens when we look for everyone—and everything—that made the moment possible?”

Elite hunting was a performance of status. The commemorative photograph helped turn that performance into public memory.

Out of Frame looks beyond the celebrated subject: toward the hosts, workers, animals, technology and political structures around the spectacle.

How to look

First, see.
Then discover.

The artwork begins without explanations. Visitors can explore the widened image, return to the source photograph, and investigate individually sourced annotations at their own pace.
